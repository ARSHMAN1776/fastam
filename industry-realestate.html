Common Questions

Real Estate FAQ

Can the AI capture leads automatically from Zillow or Realtor.com?

+
Yes. We parse inbound email notifications or set up direct webhooks from aggregator sites and instantly trigger an outbound AI SMS or voice call to the new lead in under 30 seconds — before any competitor agent can respond.

Will it sync with Follow Up Boss or GoHighLevel?

+
We specialize in real estate CRMs. Every AI interaction, transcript, and qualifying data point (budget, desired location, timeline) is automatically logged as a structured note on the contact record. No manual entry required.

Can the bot send property images and listing links to prospects?

+
Absolutely. If a prospect asks for 3-bedroom homes under $800k in a specific neighborhood, the AI queries your MLS data and replies with a curated set of matching listing links, photos, and viewing availability — all via SMS or WhatsApp.

What happens after the AI qualifies a lead?

+
Once a lead is qualified, the AI books them directly into the right agent's calendar (via Calendly or your CRM's booking system), sends a confirmation SMS to the lead, and creates a contact record with qualification notes — ready for the agent to review before the call.
