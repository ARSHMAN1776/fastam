Common Questions

Healthcare FAQ

Is this system HIPAA compliant?

+
We architect all healthcare solutions as HIPAA-aligned systems — utilizing BAA-covered endpoints, ephemeral PHI processing (never stored in the AI layer), and full audit trails. We provide architecture documentation for your compliance officer.

What if a patient reports a medical emergency during the AI interaction?

+
Our AI is trained with strict clinical liability guardrails. If it detects keywords associated with self-harm, cardiac events, severe trauma, or other emergencies, it immediately halts and directs the patient to call 911 or your on-call emergency line — and simultaneously alerts your care team.

Can the AI process insurance eligibility verification?

+
Yes. We build custom workflows that query clearinghouse APIs (Availity, Change Healthcare) to verify active patient coverage, copay requirements, and deductibles — automatically before each appointment, with results pushed to the billing system.

Which EHR systems do you integrate with?

+
We have pre-built connectors for Epic, Cerner, and Athenahealth. For other systems, we integrate via HL7 FHIR APIs. If your EHR has any kind of API capability, we can build the bridge — typically within the standard 5-day deployment window.
