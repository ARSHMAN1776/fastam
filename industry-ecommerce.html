Common Questions

E-Commerce FAQ

Can the AI handle live inventory checks and order tracking?

+
Yes. Our agents query your Shopify or WooCommerce APIs in milliseconds — providing customers with exact stock levels, real-time order status, and estimated delivery windows without any human in the loop.

Can it process returns and refunds automatically?

+
We build fully automated return workflows: the AI qualifies the return against your policy, generates a prepaid label via your 3PL (ShipBob, ShipStation), and initiates the refund sequence — no agent required.

What happens when the AI can't answer something?

+
The AI gracefully escalates to a human agent on Zendesk or Gorgias, automatically passing the full conversation transcript, customer order history, and a suggested resolution draft to speed up human handling.

Will this integrate with my existing tech stack?

+
If it has a webhook or REST API, we can connect it. We natively support Shopify, WooCommerce, Klaviyo, Gorgias, Zendesk, Shipbob, Airtable and more. Custom integrations are built as part of every engagement.
