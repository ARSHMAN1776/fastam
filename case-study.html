The Challenge

Manual Work Was Killing Growth

A growing real estate agency with 8 agents was spending 25+ hours per week manually copying lead data between their website, CRM, email platform, and Slack. Leads were falling through the cracks. Agents were frustrated. Revenue was suffering.

They needed a solution that would automatically handle lead capture, CRM entry, agent assignment, follow-up sequences and internal notifications — without adding any manual touchpoints.

Problems Before Fastam

  • 25+ hours/week wasted on manual data entry
  • Leads getting lost between systems
  • No automated follow-up sequences
  • Agents notified by email only — slow response
  • No visibility into lead pipeline status
  • CRM data was 3–4 days behind reality

The Fastam Solution Stack

  • n8n workflow: Website → CRM auto-entry
  • Smart lead scoring & auto-assignment to agents
  • 7-email drip sequence with personalization
  • Instant Slack + WhatsApp agent notifications
  • Google Sheets real-time pipeline dashboard
  • Automated appointment booking with Google Calendar
The Solution

12-Day Automation Overhaul

We built a multi-platform n8n automation system that connected their website forms, HubSpot CRM, Gmail, Slack, WhatsApp, and Google Calendar into one seamless, zero-touch pipeline.

Every new lead is now automatically scored, assigned, notified to the right agent via Slack/WhatsApp, entered into the CRM, and enrolled in a personalized email sequence — all within 30 seconds of form submission.
