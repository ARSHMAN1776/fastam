Common Questions

Pricing FAQ

What's included in the ROI Guarantee?

+

If our AI solution doesn't deliver measurable, agreed-upon results within 30 days of deployment, we continue working at no additional charge until it does. We agree on KPIs upfront so there's no ambiguity.

How do retainer projects get scoped?

+

Every month we kick off with a planning call to define the deliverables. You choose what to prioritize — automation, chatbot improvements, new AI features — and we execute. Unused capacity rolls over to the next month.

Can I get a custom quote for a specific project?

+

Absolutely. Every complex project gets a custom quote based on your specific requirements, timeline, and scope. Contact us and we'll respond within 24 hours with a detailed proposal.

How do you handle payments?

+

Retainers are billed monthly in advance. One-time projects are 50% upfront and 50% on delivery. We accept bank transfers, PayPal, and crypto. Larger enterprise projects can be broken into milestones.

Are there contracts or cancellation fees?

+

No long-term contracts. Monthly retainers are month-to-month and can be cancelled with 30 days notice. We believe in earning your business every month through results — not locking you in.

Do you offer discounts for annual commitments?

+

Yes — annual retainer commitments receive a 15% discount applied to all monthly payments. Contact us to set up annual billing.
