Common Questions

Finance & Fintech FAQ

Are your bots compliant with GDPR and SOC2?

+
Yes. We build entirely within encrypted environments using Zero Data Retention (ZDR) API configurations — meaning PII is tokenized in transit and never stored by the AI layer. We can provide architecture diagrams for your compliance team.

Can the AI connect to legacy core banking systems?

+
Through custom middleware and RPA (Robotic Process Automation), we can interface with modern API platforms (Stripe, Plaid, Finicity) and legacy SOAP/XML core banking systems. If it has an endpoint, we connect it.

How does the AI handle sensitive data like SSNs or account numbers?

+
Our data pipelines use field-level encryption and immediate tokenization on intake. The raw text (SSNs, account numbers) is never exposed to the LLM processing layer — only encrypted tokens are passed through the workflow.

Is this suitable for regulated financial institutions (banks, credit unions)?

+
Yes. We specifically design for regulated entities. Our deployment architecture includes BAA-equivalent agreements, full audit logging, and architecture documentation suitable for regulatory submissions or internal compliance reviews.
