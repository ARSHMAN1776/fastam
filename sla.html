Escalation Protocol

If you feel your ticket is not receiving the appropriate priority classification, Enterprise clients have direct access to our Lead Engineering Team via dedicated Slack Connect channels.

Scheduled Maintenance

To keep AI infrastructure secure and performant, Fastam Solutions schedules routine maintenance. We guarantee that at least 48 hours of advance notice will be provided for any planned maintenance window that may result in more than 5 minutes of localized downtime.
